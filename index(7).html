MENOCLONE / MOTORSPORTS DIVISION

MENOCLONE MOTORSPORTS

Engineering / Automotive Technology / Innovation

Engineering meets originality

Driven by innovation. Built to explore.

MENOCLONE Motorsports is the automotive engineering and technology division of MENOCLONE, exploring vehicle diagnostics, embedded electronics, intelligent systems, performance-inspired design and hands-on engineering.

Our projects connect automotive craftsmanship with modern hardware and software: OBD-II diagnostic tools, Raspberry Pi and ESP32 prototypes, vehicle data research and future-forward mobility concepts. We learn by building, testing and refining real systems.

“Keep Your Vision Further Than Your Eyesight!”

From import-car engineering studies to connected automotive technology, MENOCLONE Motorsports is an independent creative R&D initiative focused on turning curiosity into practical technical skill and original prototypes.
